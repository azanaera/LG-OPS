Freight Lifecycle Process Map

One shipment from first inquiry to month-end close, top to bottom. Step numbers 1 to 14 match the swimlane. Each step shows who acts, what paper and information they need, what comes out, and whether the platform covers it.

A. SellFrom first contact to a job we can work on
1

Inquiry

Shipper / Consignee

Needs

  • What is moving: goods, pieces, weight, size
  • From where to where, and by when
  • Trade term (Incoterm): who pays which charges
  • Ocean or air

Produces

  • A request, usually by email or phone
Outside the platformThe platform starts at the quote. No inquiry record is described.
2

Quote, accept, set up job

Sales / Customer service

Needs

  • Customer in the directory, with the party who will be billed
  • Rates: Tariff for the lane, or a Charge Package
  • Chargeable weight (air) or revenue weight (ocean)

Produces

  • Quote sent to customer
  • Customer's acceptance
  • Freight File with a system File Number
In the platformQuote moves Draft → Sent → Accepted, Rejected or Expired. Only an Accepted quote can become a file; its lines replace tariff lines. New file starts as Draft.
To confirmSales / Customer service is not one of the five platform roles. Operator or Manager does this today. Tariff data is mostly empty.
?

Customer accepts the quote?

YesQuote becomes Accepted. Continue to step 3.
No or no replyQuote becomes Rejected or Expired. No file is worked.
3

Booking request + instructions

Shipper / Consignee

Needs from the customer

  • Booking request and Shipping instructions
  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Goods codes (HS code) for each commodity
  • Dangerous goods declaration, if the goods are flagged
  • Shipper, consignee and notify party details

Produces

  • File filled in: parties, cargo, dimension lines, containers (ocean), weights, VAT
In the platformFile fields, commodity catalogue with HS code and dangerous-goods flag, ocean containers, air dimension lines. File is still Draft and editable.
To confirmWhether customer documents are attached to the file or kept elsewhere.
B. Plan and approveSpace is booked and a manager releases the file
4

Book carrier / airline space

Operations

Needs

  • Completed file from step 3
  • Carrier or airline, ports, preferred sailing or flight
  • Cut-off dates the customer can meet

Produces

  • Booking request to the carrier or airline
  • File submitted for approval
In the platformCarriers, ports, airlines in the directory. Submit checks required fields, then the file becomes Pending and nobody can edit it.
To confirmWhen staff submit: before booking, after the carrier confirms, or at another point.
5

Confirm space

Carrier / Airline

Needs

  • Booking request from step 4

Produces

  • Booking confirmation
  • Booking Number
  • Vessel or flight, and cut-offs
Outside the platformThe carrier's reference is typed on the file. It is not our File Number. The public lookup can find a shipment by it.
?

Manager approves the file?

Manager or Superadmin
ApproveFile becomes Approved. Operators can no longer change it. Billing is released.
Return, with a noteFile becomes For Correction. Operator fixes it and submits again. An image can be attached to the note.
Shipment will not happenThere is no Cancelled status today (OP-03). to confirm

The platform lets billing start as soon as the file is Approved. It does not wait for delivery.

C. Move the cargoOrigin side: collect, document, clear, load, depart
6

Pickup, receive, check

Warehouse / TruckerConsolidation: build up the load

Needs

  • Cartage order (pickup, delivery, cross-dock or exchange)
  • Pickup address, contact, cargo-ready date
  • Driver and truck, if our own

Produces

  • Cargo received and checked: pieces, actual weight, measurements
  • Delivery paper signed by the receiver (POD)
  • Milestone: cargo received
  • House air waybill rows added to the load
In the platformCartage order Open → Assigned → In Transit → Delivered. Dispatch routes with stops. POD records signer and time. Air: dimension lines give volumetric and chargeable weight.
In the platformPieces and gross weight are totalled from the house rows. The master's chargeable weight is the greater of the total gross and the total volumetric weight. It is not the sum of the houses' chargeable weights. Duplicate house numbers are flagged.
To confirmThe POD signature works only when a signature pad is connected. The warehouse receipt is not described. There is no "dispatcher" role.
7

Draft bill of lading Draft HAWB, approve

Documentation

Needs

  • Shipping instructions
  • Commercial invoice and Packing list
  • Checked weights and pieces from step 6
  • Shipper's confirmation of the draft

Produces

  • Draft house bill of lading, approved by shipper
  • Draft air waybill, approved by shipper
  • One house air waybill per shipper, approved
In the platformDocuments print from the browser with branded layouts.
To confirmThe design document lists a house bill of lading; the requirements do not. Nobody has checked layouts against carrier or legal formats. GAP-13
8

Export customs

Customs broker

Needs

  • Commercial invoice, Packing list
  • HS codes and values
  • Export permits or licences, if the goods need them
  • Authority to act for the shipper

Produces

  • Export declaration and customs release
Outside the platformNo customs filing or clearance status is described for cargo files.
To confirmVehicle files (roll-on/roll-off) have a customs-hold flag and a lien-release flag. Who sets or clears them is not defined. GAP-24
9

Master air waybill, consolidation manifest, e-messages

DocumentationAir consolidation only

Needs

  • Every house air waybill final
  • Totals checked against the airline booking
  • Customs release from step 8

Produces

  • Master air waybill (one for the whole load)
  • Consolidation manifest
  • Electronic messages to the airline
In the platformMaster record with house rows and totals.
To confirmManifest layout and electronic messages are not described.

Accept, depart, issue bill of lading air waybill

Carrier / Airline

Needs

  • Cargo delivered inside the cut-offs
  • Approved bill of lading or waybill details
  • VGM (verified gross mass) for each container
  • Container cut-offs met: documents, cargo, VGM

Produces

  • Master bill of lading
  • Air waybill
  • Master air waybill issued
  • Departure confirmed
In the platformStaff enter milestones by hand (dated, with place and note). The public can look up status by reference number.
To confirmThe public lookup returns "tracking fields" that are not listed, and file numbers follow a pattern. GAP-29

E. Bill and closeStep 14 broken into the stages the platform actually has
14

Invoice, pay vendors, close

Finance (Accountant role)
14a. Rate and invoice In the platform
  • Needs: Approved file, tariffs and charge codes, bill-to party, document currency, VAT, charge code linked to a ledger account
  • Produces: sell lines (to customer), cost lines (expected supplier costs), manual lines if typed in, then a Draft invoice with a temporary reference. Issue gives the final Invoice Number, which never changes.
  • Each save of an approved file re-rates the lines. Only one live invoice per file.
  • GAP-33: a consolidation cannot be invoiced per house customer. GAP-34: one bill-to party per file, so prepaid/collect or agent splits cannot be shown.
14b. Accounting Bridge In the platform
  • Needs: an invoice or cost item, and a ledger account for its charge code (no account means refused)
  • Produces: Bridge item: Staged → Trial Verified → Posted. Each item is staged once. Trial posting writes nothing to the books.
  • Company setting decides how invoices reach the books: Manual, Review or Auto.
14c. Ledger In the platform
  • Produces: balanced journal and voucher numbers. Posted entries are never edited.
  • If an invoice is wrong: Void it. A Reversal voucher undoes the posting and the next invoice revision is drafted.
  • AC-06: no credit note exists for a partial correction.
14d. Pay vendors In the platform To confirm
  • Needs: supplier bill, authorised signatory
  • Produces: Check disbursement voucher: Draft → Printed → Posted or Void. Staff print the check; there is no bank transfer.
  • GAP-54: cost lines are estimates. Actual supplier bills are not recorded or matched. GAP-48: "CDV" is defined two ways.
14e. Month-end close In the platform
  • Needs: every item for the month posted or deliberately left out
  • Produces: period set to Closed. Nothing more can be dated in that month, and the system cannot reopen it.
  • Also not covered: profit per file, collections (AC-08 to AC-10).

Running alongside every step

  • Tracking: milestones are typed in by staff, shown on the public lookup page and on the live map. The map needs internet and a ship-feed token; without the token no ships show. GAP-31
  • Access: five roles only: Superadmin, Manager, Operator, Accountant, Viewer. Branches exist on the map but not in permissions.
  • Returned files: For Correction → Submit → Pending → decision again. No limit on loops is described.
